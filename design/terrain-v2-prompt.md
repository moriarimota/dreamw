## 完整提示词

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production terrain texture atlas for an existing cozy witch pixel-art village game. Reference image is only a style and palette reference: match its warm, richly textured hand-painted pixel foliage, earthy ochre stone and moss. Generate a square 1024x1024 image divided into exactly four equally sized 512x512 quadrants with NO borders or gaps. Top left: seamless dense short meadow grass in muted natural olive and fern green, softly mottled small grass blades, low contrast, no flowers, no large objects. Top right: seamless warm sandy ochre worn earth path with tiny irregular pebbles, natural subtle variation, no grass. Bottom left: seamless mossy grey-brown small irregular cobblestone paving, soft worn edges, consistent top-down lighting. Bottom right: seamless shallow teal river water with delicate horizontal ripples and diffuse olive reflections, no banks. Each quadrant is a flat overhead material filling every pixel, constant scale, appropriate underneath the detailed buildings in the reference. Crisp fine pixel clusters, cohesive gentle daylight, natural painterly game textures, no perspective, no vignettes, no shadows of objects, no UI, no text, no buildings, no characters. The grass texture should feel like dense living ground instead of sparse square noise.
